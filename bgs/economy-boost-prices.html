Economy Boost Prices

Mining sell prices per faction state and state combination. Pick a material.

How to read it:
  • State = active states of the faction that owns the station. "No active state" means none.
  • Only prices updated in the last 7 days count, and only where the owner's state was read within 24 h of the price.
  • Max and Avg use only stations with demand above 1,000 t. Selling into low demand drops the price the market shows next, so a low-demand price mostly reflects someone's last sale.
  • Min = lowest price at a station in that state set with demand 0, one that does not consume the material. – when there is none.
  • Rows are listed when the max is at least 1.5× the material's median and at least 100,000 Cr.
  • Max = highest single station price. Avg = mean over those stations. × median = max divided by the material's median (demand above 1,000 t, all stations).
  • Station economy = economy of the station that paid the max. Markets = stations in that state set with demand above 1,000 t.
