Economy Boost Prices

Best mining sell prices per faction state and state combination. Pick a material.

How to read it:
  • State = active states of the faction that owns the station. "No active state" means none.
  • Only prices updated in the last 7 days count. Rows are listed when the max is at least 1.5× the galactic average and at least 100,000 Cr.
  • Max / Min = highest / lowest single station price. Avg = mean over all stations in that state set. × base = max divided by the galactic average.
  • Station economy = economy of the station that paid that price. Markets = stations in that state set listing the material.
