What the columns mean

  • Metal-Rich and High Metal - the game's own body classes, as the system map names them.
  • Volcanic Magma - a rocky body with metallic or rocky volcanism.
  • Volcanic Silicate - a rocky body with silicate volcanism. Magma and silicate carry opposite materials, which is why they are not one column: monazite reads 45.6% on magma and 7.9% on silicate, jadeite 10.5% and 55.5%.
  • Rocky - a rocky body with no volcanism.
  • Rocky Ice and Icy - the icy classes. Both rest on few locations, so read them as a hint.
  • Median and Max - sell price per tonne across the markets we hold prices for. Ega, fleet carriers, on-foot settlements and rows reading demand 999999 are left out, the last being a placeholder rather than a market.
