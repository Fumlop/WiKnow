What the columns mean

  • Every column is the game's body class as the system map names it. Only a Rocky body splits again on its volcanism: magma on a Metal-Rich or High Metal body counts under that body's column, not under a rock 80%+ magma column. The magma material rules ask rock ≥ 80%: every Rocky body read clears it, no metal body does.
  • metal-rich - Metal-rich body, with or without volcanism, metal 100% on the bodies read.
  • high-metal-content - High metal content body, with or without volcanism, metal 30-35% on the bodies read.
  • rock 80%+ [none] - Rocky body, no volcanism, rock 90-91% on the bodies read.
  • rock 80%+ [metallic magma] - Rocky body with metallic magma, rock 91% on the bodies read.
  • rock 80%+ [rocky magma] - Rocky body with rocky magma, rock 91% on the bodies read.
  • rock 80%+ [silicate vapour geysers] - Rocky body with silicate vapour geysers, rock 86-97% on the bodies read.
  • rocky-ice - Rocky ice body, rock 52% on the bodies read.
  • icy - Icy body, rock 12-18% on the bodies read.
  • Magma and silicate geysers carry opposite materials, which is why they are not one column - compare monazite and jadeite across the two.
  • Median/t and Best/t - sell price per tonne across the markets we hold prices for. Ega, fleet carriers, on-foot settlements and rows reading demand 999999 are left out, the last being a placeholder rather than a market.
